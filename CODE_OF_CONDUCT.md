# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

**Our pledge:** everyone can take part in this project and community free of harassment, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

**Expected:** be respectful and constructive; give and accept feedback graciously; focus on what is best for the community; take responsibility for mistakes.
**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, trolling, publishing others' private information, or other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

**Enforcement:** maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that violate this code and may ban contributors for behaviour they judge inappropriate. Report problems privately by opening a GitHub security advisory (Report a vulnerability → "conduct concern") or by contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
