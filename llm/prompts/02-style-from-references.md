# 02 · Style from references

**Use when:** you have videos you like and want their *language*, not a copy. **Keep the videos local; commit only the written analysis.**

```
I'm attaching N reference videos (local files). Do NOT reuse their content, logos or text.
1. Extract frames (ffmpeg, 1 per second, contact sheets) and cut points (scene detection). Look at every sheet.
2. Write what they share, as design rules: palette strategy, geometry/transition devices, typography rhythm, how product UI is shown, pacing (slow build then burst?), easing.
3. For the LAST video also analyse the sound: loudness (LUFS/LRA), low-band vs mid-band energy, silences, hit timings and tempo (measure, don't guess).
4. Map each rule onto OUR brand (docs/BRAND.md kit). Say what we copy, what we don't, and why.
Write it to docs/<project>-brief.md with sections: Shared language · Sound · Mapping to our identity.
```
